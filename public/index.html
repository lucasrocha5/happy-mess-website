How it works

Hi, I’m Allison.

For over twenty years, my 9 to 5 was corporate and my 5 to 9 was splashes of paint and glints of jewelry making with my children. Happy Mess is a love letter to my creative side and an invitation for you and your family to find yours.

Frequently asked
questions

Have something you want to clarify? What you're looking for might be in these FAQs. If not, please don't hesitate to contact us.

What ages do you cater for?

If you’re old enough to paint, you’re old enough to get creative. Happy Mess is for all ages.

How many people can attend?

The party price covers up to 12 people. If you’re hosting a larger group, it’s $50 for every extra person.

How long does a party run for?

Two hours including setup.

Where does it happen?

In the comfort of your own home. Alternatively, we have a dedicated room in a cafe in Greenwich, Connecticut, that we can book directly and pass the cost along to you. Whatever the location, we bring everything with us to take the load off your plate (so you can replace it with birthday cake instead).

What about the mess?

Paint is washable, the mess is half the fun anyway. That’s where our name comes from.

What's included?

Setup, materials for one pottery piece per child, and firing. Larger pieces, or more than one each, come at an extra cost. You can add party favors and delivery of the finished pottery when you book.

What about for the grown-ups?

If you’re planning a Craft & Sip night with the girls or a casual gathering with friends, we’ll supply some liquid creativity along with everything else (see above FAQ and visit our “For Grown-Ups” page.)

How far in advance should I book?

Two weeks. Feel free to contact us to see if we can squeeze you in earlier if there’s a cancellation.

How long does the firing process take?

About a week, depending on the size of the party.
