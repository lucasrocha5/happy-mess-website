Our story

Why I traded taking board calls for making pottery and helping people fall back in love with painting.

Hi, I’m Allison

For over twenty years, I worked in corporate. For as long as I can remember, I’ve been creative.

Getting creative with my kids has always been one of my favorite things to do. Whether we were painting, making jewelry, or trying out a new arts and crafts project, it was always about having fun, spending time together, and creating something special. What started as a fun family activity eventually grew into participating in craft fairs and sharing our creations with others.

Opening a pottery studio has been a dream of mine for as long as I can remember. I’ve always loved visiting pottery studios, picking out something fun to paint, and getting lost in the creativity of it all. Every time we’d visit one, I’d find myself thinking,

“One day, I’d love to have a place like this of my own.”

After years of talking about it, dreaming about it, and imagining all the possibilities, one day we finally said, “Maybe now is the time!”

And just like that, Happy Mess started coming to life. What began as a dream is turning into something special, a place where creativity, laughter, and a little bit of mess all come together. Because sometimes, all you need is a little paint, a little imagination, and the freedom to make something completely your own.

Why “mess”

We went through a few names but somehow kept coming back to it. Paint washes off but the mess you make is a reminder of the creative process. That’s why our logo is a pink bowl with a smile on it.

A studio is in the works

Right now, Happy Mess is on the move. One day, it’ll have a home of its own. Drop your email and you’ll be the first to know when our studio opens.
