How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a date

    Tell us the when and the where, plus how many kids you’re expecting at the party. We’ll be in touch to confirm.

  2. 2

    We arrive and set up

    The pottery, paint, and brushes arrive and leave with us, so there’s nothing for you to prep or clean after the party.

  3. 3

    Let’s get creative

    Every child paints their own piece as creatively as they’d like.

  4. 4

    Into the fire

    We take the pieces to our kiln to get fired. Add delivery to your booking and we’ll bring each finished piece back to you.
