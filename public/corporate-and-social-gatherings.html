How to book with us

  1. 1

    Get in contact

    Tell us where, when and roughly how many people you’re expecting.

  2. 2

    We arrive and set up

    We’ll bring the pottery, paints and brushes. Setup is part of the two and a half hours.

  3. 3

    Everyone picks up a brush

    No skill level required. If you’re painting a piece of pottery, you’re doing it right.

  4. 4

    We fire the pieces

    We take your pieces to the kiln, then bring the finished pottery back to you in about a week.

Good to know

How many people can attend?

Both packages include pottery and materials for up to 15 people. Extra guests are $50 each.

How long does the event last?

Two and a half hours, with setup baked in.

Are alcoholic drinks allowed?Craft & Sip

We go one step further: we supply a small selection for your group to sip on while painting.

Can you come to my office?Corporate Crafting

Absolutely! Fill in our inquiry form to let us know where to go.

How long does the firing process take?

Usually about a week, depending on the size of your group.

How do I get everyone’s finished pieces to them?

You can choose to add delivery to your booking. We’ll drop the finished pieces off at your home (Craft & Sip) or office (Corporate Crafting).
